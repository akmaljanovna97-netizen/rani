For Rani

Dear Rani,

If these words ever find their way to you, I hope they arrive quietly. They were never meant to ask anything of you. Some thoughts simply become too heavy to remain unspoken. I used to believe people changed our lives through extraordinary moments. I was wrong. It was the ordinary conversations, the careless laughter, and the little moments you probably forgot that quietly stayed with me.

I don't know where life will take you or whose hand you'll choose to hold. I only hope they notice the kindness you carry, the quiet strength you hide, and the smile you never realize is beautiful. As for me... I'll always be grateful that our paths crossed, even if only for a brief moment. Not every story is meant to be lived.

Some are simply meant to be remembered. And somehow... I think ours will always remain my favorite unfinished chapter. Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. And... ❤️
